03

How I make an AI film

Every film is planned on paper first, so the faces, the light and the story stay the same from shot to shot.

5 steps
  1. 01

    Story and shot plan

    The idea, a short script and a shot list with timing, lens and camera move for every shot.

  2. 02

    Characters and look

    Faces, clothes and places are locked first, so every shot matches the last.

  3. 03

    Storyboard frames

    A still image for each shot, picked from several takes before any video is made.

  4. 04

    Image to video

    Each frame is animated with the video model that suits the shot best.

  5. 05

    Edit, sound and colour

    Cut in Premiere Pro, titles in After Effects, voice and music timed to every move.

04

Shot-by-shot plans with lens, camera move and timing, and key frames locked before any video is made. The faces are AI stand-ins.
